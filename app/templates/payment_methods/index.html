Tokenization & Security Invariants

  • Tokenized Identifiers: Payment sources are represented by opaque secure tokens (tok_...), keeping financial credentials off the server.
  • Zero CVV Storage: CVVs are never requested, stored, or accepted under any circumstances.
  • Masked Display: Only the last 4 digits are retained (e.g. **** **** **** 4242 or Bank Acct: *******5678).
  • AES-256-GCM Encryption: Sensitive metadata (bank routing notes, billing addresses) is encrypted at rest.
  • IDOR Boundary: Users can only access and manage their own payment methods.
